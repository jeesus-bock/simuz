**Culture:** Artistic, magical, patient, reclusive. Build harmonious cities in forests, mountains, or magical sanctuaries.

#### High Elf
+1 INT, learns one extra skill at creation. Graceful, magical. Cities of crystal and stone.

#### Wood Elf
+1 WIS, +1 Stealth. Dresses in natural tones, lives in forest harmony. Faster in natural terrain (+1 speed in forests).

#### Dark Elf (Drow)
+1 CHA, Darkvision 120ft, Sunlight Sensitivity. Lives in cavern cities. Matriarchal, worship spider deities. Poison-resistant.

#### Sea Elf
+1 CON, Swim speed 8, Amphibious (breathe air and water). Crystal cities beneath the waves.

#### Snow Elf
+1 CON, Cold Resistance 5, +1 Stealth (snow). Pale skin, white hair. Lives in arctic regions.
